Season's Greetings from San Diego, December 2026

It's that time of year again! Time to catch you up on what has been going on in my life this past year.

I am happily still teaching science at High Tech Elementary. This is my 4th year at the school and it currently looks good for me to stay here until I retire (hopefully in 2030). This year, I added science with the TK students (pre-Kindergarten) which has been a lot of fun. Unfortunately, San Diego County suddenly decided that in order to teach science in the elementary school I now need a specialist credential, so I am back in the classroom taking night classes. I also had to purchase a termporary permit to continue teaching in the meantime, but hopefully I will be done with all this by the end of this school year. There's always something!

Lisa continues to do a great job teaching history at Rancho Buena Vista High School. This year she moved to live closer to where she works which has helped enormously with how much time she spends on the road each week. She is still less than an hour's drive away from me so I get to see her fairly frequently, particularly since her boyfriend, Neil, lives close by me ;)

Teddy and Sky are still my crazy pups. They have calmed down a little now they are 5? years old, and are slightly more able to deal with people and dogs on our walks. However, they still have trouble dealing with newe visitors to the house.

My social world is still split between friends from Irish dance, run club (West Coast Road Runners), and pickleball (San Diego Pickleball). I don't dance any more, nor do I do much running these days, but I am very thankful to have retained great friends from both of these groups.

I have recently joined a local group that is made up of women over 50 that like to gather for outdoor activities. So far, I have joined them for hiking, paddleboarding, and dancing events, and hope to soon add some biking and boogie boarding. San Diego is an ideal place for everything outdoors and I love to take advantage of that whenever I can!

I am so grateul to have friends who like to go to the theatre. This year I have again been to events at the Old Globe, San Diego Civic Theatre, Cygnet Theatre, and Rady Shell. I also attended my first local soccer game at Snapdragon Stadium. These are so much more fun to do with friends!

I did not travel much this year. In May, a group of us went to Paso Robles for an epic wine tasting tour. I love the wines from there and can't believe I had never visited before.

In November, a group of us went to Mexico City to coincide with their annual Día de los Muertos parade. It was my first time visiting this city and I loved it. The people, the food, and the variety of neighborhoods! I also loved all the art and music everywhere we went. As a bonus we got to see the world's biggest Catrina!

All in all, it has been another good year! Great friends, fun times, a rewarding job, and a healthy happy life!

Wishing you all a very happy holiday season! Love, Jackie
